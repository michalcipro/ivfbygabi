What BlooMia is and what it is not

BlooMia is a digital app for information and record keeping. It is there so that you can record the course of your own treatment and have in one place the educational content that relates to the stage and the day you entered yourself.

BlooMia is not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745 on medical devices, nor within the meaning of Czech Act No. 375/2022 Coll. Nor is it the provision of health services under Czech Act No. 372/2011 Coll.

In particular, the app:

  • does not make a diagnosis,
  • does not determine or change treatment,
  • does not determine or change medication doses,
  • does not evaluate lab values as good or bad,
  • does not predict the outcome of treatment and does not calculate individual chances of success,
  • does not send any alert to a doctor or a clinic,
  • does not replace a doctor, a clinic or medical care.

The content of the app is general and educational. It does not constitute individual medical advice and no conclusions about your situation can be drawn from it. For decisions about treatment, what your treating doctor or clinic says always applies. The details are in the medical disclaimer, which forms an integral part of these terms.

By concluding the contract you acknowledge that you use the app alongside medical care, not instead of it, and that the Provider gives no warranty as to the outcome of treatment.

All records in the app are entered by the user herself. The Provider does not check or verify them and the app works with them exactly as entered.

Your records and backups

Your records are stored solely on your device, in the browser's storage. They are not sent to the Provider's server and the Provider has no access to them. The app requires no user account and no password.

One important thing follows from that: if you clear your browser data, uninstall the browser, lose the device or have it wiped, the records are gone and the Provider has nowhere to restore them from. That is not a malfunction; it is the consequence of your not sending the data to anyone.

The app therefore offers a backup to a file and loading it back. The backup contains everything you have written and can be locked with a password. Creating the backup, storing it safely and protecting it from third parties is the user's responsibility.

The backup feature also gives effect to the right to obtain the content you created after the contract ends, under Section 2389o of the Czech Civil Code. Because the data is with you the whole time, there is nothing to hand over: saving a backup is enough.
