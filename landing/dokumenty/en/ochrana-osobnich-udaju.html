The main thing, in one sentence

Everything you write into BlooMia stays stored on your own device. It is not sent to a server and the controller has no access to it.

The app requires no registration, no account and no password. It uses no measurement or analytics tools and shows no advertising.

The rest of this document explains what follows from that, and describes the few situations where some data is processed after all: when you write to us yourself, when you take out a subscription, and when you simply open the website.

Who the controller is

The controller of personal data is Gabriela Cipro, company ID 21134154, registered at V Jezerech 311, Braškov 273 51, Czech Republic, e-mail hello@mybloomia.com.

Processing is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and by Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll., on personal data processing.

No data protection officer has been appointed. The obligation under Article 37(1) GDPR does not arise: the controller is not a public authority, her core activity does not consist of large-scale regular and systematic monitoring, nor of large-scale processing of special categories of data. The controller does not process data about your treatment at all; it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

What you record in the app

In the app you can record, for example, your name or a nickname, the stage of your treatment, cycles, retrievals, embryos, transfers, medication, lab values, mood, diary entries, costs, documents and photographs.

This data is stored using your browser's own storage, directly on your device:

The controller does not collect, transmit or see this data. It is not subject to processing on her side unless you send it yourself in one of the ways described below.

Health data

Data about fertility treatment falls within the special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR. That is precisely why the app is built so that this data never leaves your device.

If you send such data to us yourself, for example in the text of your feedback, it is processed on the basis of your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) GDPR, and solely for the purpose of dealing with your message.

Please do not write sensitive health details into the feedback form unless they are necessary to answer your question.

Feedback and contact

If you use the feedback form in the app, only the following is sent:

  • the topic you chose,
  • your name, if you fill it in (optional),
  • your e-mail address, and only if you tick the box saying you would like a reply,
  • the star rating, if you give one,
  • the date it was sent,
  • the text you write yourself.

Diary entries, cycles, lab values and photographs are never sent through the form.

How the message leaves. When no forwarding service is set up, the app only opens your own e-mail program with the message pre-filled, and you send it yourself. In that case the controller uses no technical service at all and the message goes straight from your mailbox.

No forwarding service is set up. The message therefore goes straight from your mailbox and the controller uses no processor for it. Should that ever change, the name and registered address of the processor will be added here, and this policy will be updated before the service is switched on.

Purpose and legal basis. Dealing with your message, that is performance of a contract or the legitimate interest in communicating with users (Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR), and for sensitive data the explicit consent described in article 4.

Running the site and hosting

Both the website and the app run with the hosting provider Vercel Inc., registered in the United States of America. It may briefly process technical data in its operational logs, for example the IP address, the browser type and the time of access. This serves to keep the service running and secure (legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The hosting provider keeps operational logs for a short time under its own policy, which you can read at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy. As this is a provider outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is governed by the standard contractual clauses under Article 46 GDPR, which form part of the data processing agreement concluded with that provider.

Cookies and similar technologies

BlooMia uses no cookies for traffic measurement, profiling or advertising. No analytics or marketing tools are in place.

The app uses the browser's technical storage, that is localStorage and IndexedDB. That is not a cookie and does not serve to track you. Without it the app could not work, because that is exactly where your records are kept. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

The app can be added to your phone's home screen. So that it works without a connection, your browser caches its files. None of your records are in that cache.

If a measurement tool were ever put in place, consent would be requested beforehand and this article would change.

Backups and moving to another device

The app can save all your data into a backup file. That file is created on your device and is under your control.

The backup contains everything you have written, photographs included, and it is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere safe and think about where you share it. Folders such as Downloads are often synchronised to the cloud automatically.

The controller has no access to backups and does not keep them.

Who the data is shared with

Data is not shared for marketing purposes and is not sold. The set of recipients is limited to what the service could not run without:

  • the hosting provider Vercel Inc., United States of America, see article 7,
  • the payment service provider Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, Ireland, see article 6,
  • public authorities, where the law requires it.

By contrast, data is not shared:

  • with a forwarding service for the feedback form, because none is set up and the message goes straight from your own mailbox,
  • with anyone for marketing purposes.

The only transfer outside the European Economic Area on the controller's side is the one connected with hosting. Its legal basis is given in article 7. None of your records from the app are transferred this way, because they are never sent anywhere at all.

Your rights

In relation to data the controller processes, you have the right:

  • of access to the data and to a copy of it,
  • to rectification of inaccurate data,
  • to erasure,
  • to restriction of processing,
  • to data portability,
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out so far,
  • to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, www.uoou.cz, or with the supervisory authority in your own country.

For records stored on your device you exercise these rights directly: the data is with you, you can correct it, export it to a backup or delete it at any time, without having to ask anyone.

Complete erasure also means clearing your browser data for this site, because deleting content inside the app may not remove every technical record from your device's storage.

How to exercise your rights

Write to hello@mybloomia.com. You will get a reply without undue delay, at the latest within one month of the request arriving.

Because the app has no user accounts, your identity cannot be verified from records in the app. For requests concerning data held by the controller, you may need to show that the data really is yours, for example from the e-mail address you wrote from.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be amended, in particular when the technical solution or the law changes. The current wording is always available at this address. You will be told about substantial changes by a message in the app, and if the controller has your e-mail, by e-mail as well.
