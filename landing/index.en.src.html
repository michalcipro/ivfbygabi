What makes BlooMia different

BlooMia knows where you are on your journey.

This is not a database of fertility articles sorted alphabetically. BlooMia knows which stage and which day you entered, and picks the content that is relevant to you today.

And it works from your most recent information, not automatically from your first cycle. If you have three cycles and nine transfers behind you, it counts from the latest one.

A concrete example

You are not just „after transfer“.

A general app knows you had a transfer. That is all it knows. BlooMia also counts how old the embryo was when it was transferred.

The day after transfer is not enough on its own. What matters too is how old the embryo was. BlooMia tells the two apart and chooses what to show you today accordingly.

This is context, not a prediction. The number of days tells you nothing about how the transfer turned out.

Four steps

How does BlooMia work?

  1. You tell it where you are.

    Pick your current situation and stage. It takes under a minute and you can change it any time.

  2. You record your journey.

    Cycles, retrievals, embryos, transfers, medication, results and more. As much or as little as you want.

  3. BlooMia picks up from your latest information.

    It does not fall back to your first cycle when you have a later one that matters more.

  4. Every day you see what makes sense today.

    The content follows the stage and the day you entered. You do not have to go looking.

Waiting for hCG

The two-week wait. Every day is different.

Day 4 after a day-3 embryo is not the same as day 4 after a blastocyst. BlooMia tells the day after transfer and the embryo age apart.

  • ◇what may be happening with the embryo
  • ◇what may be happening in your body
  • ◇what you may be feeling
  • ◇what you do not have to deal with today
  • ◇how long is left until the hCG test

None of this tells you whether the transfer worked. Only the blood test does.

Honesty instead of reading signs

Symptoms are not evidence. Not when there are many, and not when there are none.

Symptoms can come from the hormones, from ordinary changes in your body, or from pregnancy. On their own they are not a reliable way to tell how a transfer turned out.

That is why „no symptoms“ is always a valid answer in BlooMia. Nobody here will suggest that every sensation means something.

When it does not work out

When it does not work out, BlooMia stays with you.

A negative hCG is not the end of the app.
A cancelled transfer is not a system error.
A chemical pregnancy is not the same as a negative test.
A loss is not an empty space.

A cycle in BlooMia closes with one of nine outcomes. Each one has its own content, its own words and its own way forward:

ongoing pregnancy negative hCG chemical pregnancy ectopic pregnancy pregnancy loss no embryo to transfer cycle cancelled embryos frozen

Nobody here will tell you that you have to carry on or try again.

Safety

When waiting is the wrong choice, BlooMia says so.

Some daily cards tell you when to call the clinic rather than wait until morning.

  • ◇heavy bleeding
  • ◇severe or sudden pain

Questions

What people ask most.

Do I have to sign up?
No. No account, no password, no e-mail. You open the app and you are in.
Is BlooMia an app from the app store?
No, it runs in the browser. You can add it to your home screen, and then it behaves like any other app: its own icon, full screen.
Does it work offline?
Yes. After the first visit it stores itself and needs no connection after that. It works in a waiting room with no signal.
Is it only for IVF?
IVF is the centre of it, but the journey starts earlier. The content also covers the time when you are still deciding or waiting for tests.
What if my transfer did not work?
Then you are exactly who that part was written for. A negative result has its own content in BlooMia, not an empty screen and not a nudge towards the next round.
What if I am going through a loss?
Loss, chemical pregnancy and ectopic pregnancy are separate outcomes with their own content. Nobody will push you forward.
Can anyone else see what I write?
No. Your records stay in your browser's storage and are never sent to a BlooMia server. The only thing that leaves is what you send yourself through the feedback form.
Can I move my data to another phone?
Yes, through a backup. You save a file with all your data and load it on the other device. Keep that file safe: everything you have written is in it.
Does BlooMia replace a doctor?
No. It does not diagnose, it does not judge your results and it does not change your doses. For treatment decisions, what your clinic says always applies.
